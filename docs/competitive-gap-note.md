# Competitive gap refresh — dual-mode Auto + Custom

**Project:** speaker-master  
**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** Docs-only refresh against Spectra / Reflect / eqMac / SoundSource  
**Earlier baseline:** `docs/reference-landscape.md` (store) + phase-1 improvements list  
**Question:** Does anything now cleanly own **simple measured Auto + easy pro Custom** as one Mac consumer product?

---

## Verdict (unchanged)

**Dual-mode gap still open.** No surveyed Mac product ships our locked shape: toddler-simple **Auto (Measure | Narrow)** plus a progressive **pro Custom** console on the **same** system-wide engine, with cut-first safety and per-output persistence as the brand — not a side feature.

Listening evidence (user trial) can still kill or reshape the bet; this note only refreshes the **competitive** side of the gate.

## What would close the gap (pivot triggers)

Refresh again or pivot if **any** of these become true:

1. **Spectra** (or peer) ships a polished **one-button Measure Auto** *and* a clearly layered easy-pro Custom home that matches SoundSource clarity — marketed as one dual-mode consumer product.  
2. **eqMac** adds credible **speaker Measure Auto** (not headphone AutoEQ only) with safe defaults and keeps Expert depth.  
3. User listening trial shows **casual EQ (B) ≥ measured (C)** on both Sony and Alexa — measured Auto thesis weakens regardless of competitor map.

None of (1)–(2) are confirmed as of this note. (3) remains **user-run** via `docs/listening-trial-guide.md`.
